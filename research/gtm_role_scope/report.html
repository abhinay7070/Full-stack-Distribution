8-o · PMF evidence report · full population · 2026-11-08

Founders are hiring a pipeline engine that learns. 8-o is that hire.

We read every GTM job posting in our database: 6,183 postings from 1,599 ICP companies, and compared them to 30,399 postings from the rest of the swept job market. Every responsibility line (841,325 in total) was labelled twice, once by a keyword taxonomy and once by a text-embedding model, and grouped bottom-up with no taxonomy at all. This page shows what founders ask for, how sure we are, and what 8-o should build.

So what: the largest bundle is the pipeline engine (ICP → messaging → lists → outbound → reply → qualify → book → CRM), asked for in four of five core postings. Next come systems, data and measurement, then experiments and growth channels, then strategy and playbook. Together those are 8-o's Brain, Radar, Voice, Reply, Lab and Foundation. Closing is the only large bundle 8-o should not take on.

So what: no single role title covers the whole loop. SDRs carry find, reach and reply. Growth and demand gen carry decide and improve. GTM engineers and RevOps carry systems. A founder at 3–35 people cannot hire all of them, and that is the gap 8-o fills: one agent across every column.

Directional blueprint

What to target, what to build, what to leave

Promise
"Qualified pipeline on your calendar every week, without a growth hire. 8-o builds the playbook, runs it, and tells you on Friday what worked." The founder runs the demo and closes.
Buyer
Technical founder, seed to Series A, 3–35 people, still selling, has just raised or is about to post a growth / founding GTM / SDR role.
Best buying signal
An open core growth-hire posting. 410 A/B-tier companies in our database have one today; 164 of those are founding / first-hire roles. These are 8-o's Client #0 outbound list.
Price anchor
Founding GTM median base $144K, core growth hire $122K. $3–5K a month is 24–40% of a $122K salary before employer costs, with no ramp and no knowledge loss.
Main metric
Qualified meetings held and pipeline created per bet. Secondary: reply → positive → booked → held conversion, human minutes per meeting (Manual Log).

What would prove this wrong (kill criteria, 60 days)

  • Founders who are actively posting a growth role decline a paid pilot because they want a person who closes. Track the decline reason on every no.
  • Qualified-meeting rate per 100 approved sends stays below the founder's own outbound baseline after three bet cycles.
  • Founders edit more than half of drafts heavily by week 4 (founder taste is not being learned).
  • 8-o cannot book qualified demos for 8-o from the 410-company list (blueprint: Client #0 test).
Confidence

How the numbers were produced, and how far to trust them

CheckResult
PopulationAll 6,183 ICP GTM postings (1,599 companies with ≥ 1 usable posting) and all 30,399 other GTM postings from 6,416 job boards. No sampling.
Lines read841,325 lines, 546,431 unique, every one embedded and labelled.
Two labelling instrumentsKeyword taxonomy (48 duties) and an embedding classifier trained on it, scored out-of-fold with 5-fold cross-validation grouped by company. Median AUC vs keywords 0.958 (min 0.740). Duty rankings agree: Spearman ρ 0.931.
Coverage51.7% of ICP responsibility lines carry at least one duty (keywords alone: 54.9%). The rest is mostly location, culture and language text (see themes below).
Ranking stability1,000 company-level bootstrap resamples. 12 of 13 Must-have duties stay Must-have in ≥ 95% of resamples. Each duty's 95% interval is shown in the scope list.
Robust differencesA segment difference is called robust only if both instruments agree in direction with |z| ≥ 3. First hire vs later: 11 duties. Team ≤ 35 vs rest: 7 duties.
Bottom-up checkK-means on all 57,584 unique ICP responsibility lines, k = 80 (best Davies–Bouldin of k ∈ 20–80). Themes with no matching duty are listed below for review.
LimitsPostings state what founders say they want, not what the hire ends up doing. Headcount is known for a minority of companies. The classifier learns from keyword seeds, so a duty the taxonomy never names can only show up in the bottom-up themes.
