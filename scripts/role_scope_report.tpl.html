Founders are hiring a pipeline engine that learns. 8-o is that hire.

We read every GTM job posting in our database: {{N_POST}} postings from {{N_COMP}} ICP companies, and compared them to {{N_MKT}} postings from the rest of the swept job market. Every responsibility line ({{N_LINES}} in total) was labelled twice, once by a keyword taxonomy and once by a text-embedding model, and grouped bottom-up with no taxonomy at all. This page shows what founders ask for, how sure we are, and what 8-o should build.

What would prove this wrong (kill criteria, 60 days)

  • Founders who are actively posting a growth role decline a paid pilot because they want a person who closes. Track the decline reason on every no.
  • Qualified-meeting rate per 100 approved sends stays below the founder's own outbound baseline after three bet cycles.
  • Founders edit more than half of drafts heavily by week 4 (founder taste is not being learned).
  • 8-o cannot book qualified demos for 8-o from the {{AB}}-company list (blueprint: Client #0 test).
